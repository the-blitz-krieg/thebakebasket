FAQs

Questions people ask

Where is The Bake Basket?

The Bake Basket is a home bakery at 229, 1F, AP Achalendra Apartment, Madhavaram Red Hills Highways, Madhavaram, Chennai 600060. It's run by founder and baker Priya Shell.

Do you use ready mixes?

No. Cakes are made from scratch, with real butter, good chocolate and honest flour, and baked fresh after you order.

What allergens should I mention when I order?

Tell us about any allergy to eggs, nuts, peanuts, gluten, dairy or soy. The kitchen handles all of these, so Priya will explain honestly what she can and can't guarantee.

Is the eggless range made in a separate kitchen?

No. Eggless bakes are made in the same home kitchen that uses eggs, with separate bowls and careful washing. For severe allergies or strict observance, talk to Priya before ordering.

Can I add a personal message?

Yes. Cakes can carry a short piped message, and longer words fit on the handwritten note that comes with your order.

What if it rains on my delivery day?

Chennai rain happens. Orders are packed to travel safely, and if heavy rain affects a delivery we'll message you before the agreed time, not after.

Do you bake for events and pop-ups?

Yes, when the date allows. Email hello@thebakebasket.com with the event, date and number of guests.

Who writes the blog?

Priya Shell, Prisha Shell and Beth Mathew write the blog: baking know-how, planning guides, festival foods and gifting ideas.

Ready when you are

Tell me the occasion, the date and how many people. I'll take it from there.
