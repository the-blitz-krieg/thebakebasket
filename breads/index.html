Fresh breads

Bread that makes the whole flat smell like home

There's a particular sound a good crust makes when the knife goes through it. A soft crackle, then quiet as the blade reaches the crumb. You don't hear it with a packet loaf, and once you've heard it, you start to miss it.

My breads are made slowly, with time to rise properly, and baked after you order. Flour, water, yeast or starter, salt, and a little time. That's most of it.

FAQs

Questions people ask

How long does homemade bread last?

Without preservatives, homemade bread is best within two to three days. Keep it in a bread bag or cloth at room temperature, or slice and freeze it and toast straight from the freezer.

Is sourdough healthier than regular bread?

Sourdough's long fermentation can make it easier for some people to digest and gives it a lower glycaemic impact than many white breads, but it is still bread. It's a better everyday choice, not a health food.

Should I keep bread in the fridge in Chennai?

The fridge dries bread out faster. In Chennai's humidity, keep it in a cool, dry spot for a day or two, and freeze anything you won't eat by then.
