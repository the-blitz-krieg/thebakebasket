Bento cakes

Bento cakes: a small cake for a big feeling

Some feelings are too small for a 1 kg cake and too big for a text message.

The first anniversary when it's just the two of you. The friend who got the job. The night after the exams. Your sister moving into her first flat, with no plates yet and two forks borrowed from the neighbour.

Order a bento cakeSee the menu

That's what a bento cake is for. A little cake, about the size of your palm and a half, in a lunchbox with a spoon tucked in and a few words piped across the top. Enough for two to four people, or one person and a very good evening.

Why a small cake works so well

There's no leftover cake going stale in the fridge for a week. It's easy to carry on a bike. It fits on the desk at work. And because it's small, you can choose the flavour you actually love instead of the one that pleases twenty people.

It's also become the way Chennai says "I thought of you". People send them across the city for no occasion at all, which may be the nicest reason there is.

Flavours

Chocolate truffle, red velvet, butterscotch, vanilla with berries, Biscoff, salted caramel, filter-coffee mocha, rasmalai and pistachio rose. Most can be made eggless.

What to write on top

Short wins. The top of a bento is small, so five or six words look best. A few that people love:

  • "Happy 1st, us."
  • "You did it!"
  • "Sorry. Cake?"
  • "Best Amma ever."
  • "Ok fine, you're right."
  • "Welcome home."

FAQs

Questions people ask

How many people does a bento cake serve?

A bento cake is a small cake of roughly 300 to 400 g, enough for two to four people.

What is a bento cake?

A bento cake is a small cake packed in a lunchbox-style container, usually with a short message piped on top. The idea started in South Korea and is now one of the most popular small gifts in Chennai.

Can I get an eggless bento cake?

Yes, most bento flavours can be made eggless. Mention it when you order on WhatsApp.

Can I send a bento cake to someone as a surprise?

Yes. Give us the recipient's address and a delivery window, and we'll deliver it with a handwritten Basket Note.

How should I store a bento cake?

Keep it in the fridge in its box and take it out about 15 minutes before eating. It's best eaten within a day or two.
