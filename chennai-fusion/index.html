Chennai fusion

Flavours from home, baked into cake

Some flavours taste like Chennai before you've even swallowed. Filter coffee, strong and a little bitter, the kind that comes in a steel tumbler and dabara at seven in the morning. Tender coconut on a hot afternoon. Rasmalai at a wedding. Gulab jamun at every function you've ever been to.

So I bake them into cakes and cupcakes. It started as an experiment for my own family. It turned into some of the bakes people ask for most.

Order a fusion bakeSee the menu

Filter-coffee cake

Real decoction, folded into a soft coffee sponge and a coffee cream that tastes like the top of a freshly poured tumbler. It isn't sweet in the way a mocha from a café is sweet. It's for people who like their coffee strong and their cake grown-up. Pairs beautifully with an evening filter coffee, which feels a little like cheating.

Tender coconut cake

Light, milky and cool, with soft pieces of tender coconut through the cream. It's the cake I'd choose for a May birthday, when anything heavy feels like too much.

Rasmalai and gulab jamun cakes

For the family that wants cake and mithai and refuses to choose. The rasmalai cake is soaked with saffron milk and scented with cardamom. The gulab jamun cake hides pieces of jamun in the layers, so every slice has a little surprise.

Kesar pista and pistachio rose

Saffron and pistachio for Deepavali and weddings; pistachio and rose for anniversaries and anyone who likes something floral and gentle.

FAQs

Questions people ask

What does filter-coffee cake taste like?

Filter-coffee cake tastes like strong South Indian filter coffee with milk: rich, slightly bitter and only gently sweet. It's made with real coffee decoction in both the sponge and the cream.

Is rasmalai cake very sweet?

Rasmalai cake is soaked in saffron milk, so it's moist and fragrant. It's sweeter than a plain sponge but lighter than most mithai. Ask for reduced sugar if you prefer.

Can fusion cakes be made eggless?

Yes. Most Chennai fusion flavours, including filter coffee, rasmalai and tender coconut, can be made eggless.

Which fusion flavour is best for a birthday?

For adults, filter-coffee and pistachio rose are lovely. For a mixed family crowd, rasmalai and gulab jamun cakes are usually the ones that disappear first.
