How to order

How ordering works

Ordering from a home baker should feel like messaging a friend who happens to bake, not like filling in a form. Three steps, all on WhatsApp.

Message Priya on WhatsAppSee the menu

1. Message on WhatsApp

Tell me three things: the occasion, the date, and roughly how many people. If you have pictures of designs you like, send them along. If you're not sure what you want, say so. That's the fun part.

2. Plan it together

We settle the flavour, the size, the date and any diet needs: eggless, reduced sugar, millet, no maida, nut-free for school. I'll tell you honestly what will work and what won't, and then I'll confirm the price in writing, so there are no surprises later.

3. Baked fresh on the day

Your order is baked for you, not before you order and not for anyone else. Then it's ready for pickup from our kitchen in Madhavaram, or delivered by arrangement.

How far ahead to order

Please order cakes 2 days ahead. That gives the sponge time to bake and cool properly, and the decoration time to be done with care.

For tiered and detailed fondant cakes, gift hampers in quantity and festival orders such as kozhukattai, plum cake or Deepavali baskets, message me as early as you can, ideally a week ahead. The oven and the steamer only hold so much, and festival mornings arrive for everyone at once.

Last minute? Message anyway. If I can do it properly, I will. If I can't, I'll tell you, rather than rush something that matters to you.

Pickup and delivery

Our kitchen is at 229, 1F, AP Achalendra Apartment, Madhavaram Red Hills Highways, Madhavaram, Chennai 600060. Pickup and delivery are arranged with each order. North Chennai neighbourhoods are closest; for other parts of the city, tell me your area and we'll work out what's possible. Delivery areas

We're open Monday to Saturday. We're closed on Sundays.

When your order arrives

Put cream and buttercream cakes straight into the fridge, and take them out about 20 minutes before cutting. Brownies and cookies are happy in an airtight box. Steamed foods are best the same day. If you're carrying a cake in a car, keep it flat on the floor with the AC on, never on a seat or a lap. Read: How to Keep a Cake Fresh in Chennai Heat
