FAQs

Questions people ask

Which parts of Chennai do you deliver to?

The Bake Basket is based in Madhavaram and bakes for North Chennai first: Madhavaram, Moolakadai, Kolathur, Kodungaiyur, Puzhal, Manali, Perambur, Red Hills and nearby areas. West and Central Chennai, including Villivakkam, Anna Nagar and Ambattur, are arranged order by order, and further areas by arrangement for larger orders.

Is there a home baker near me in North Chennai?

The Bake Basket is a home bakery in Madhavaram, North Chennai, close to Moolakadai, Kolathur, Kodungaiyur, Puzhal and Red Hills. Message on WhatsApp to check your area.

Which bakes travel best over longer distances?

Brownies, cookies, breads, plum cakes, buttercream and ganache cakes travel best. Fresh cream cakes are lovely nearby, or timed for cooler hours on longer drives.
