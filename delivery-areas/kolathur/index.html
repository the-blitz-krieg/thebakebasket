FAQs

Kolathur questions

Do you deliver birthday cakes to Kolathur?

Yes. Kolathur is about 5 km from our kitchen in Madhavaram, and delivery is arranged with each order.

Can you make eggless cakes for a Kolathur party?

Yes. Most cakes, cupcakes, brownies and cookies can be made eggless. Mention it when you order.

Which frosting works best for a party hall in Kolathur?

If the hall has a fridge, fresh cream is lovely. If not, buttercream or ganache holds up better until cutting time.
