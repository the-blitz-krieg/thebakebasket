Corporate orders

Freshly made for your people, on time

If you're the person who organises the office birthdays, you know the job isn't really about cake. It's about the 3.30 pm moment when the whole floor gathers and you're quietly hoping it arrives on time, tastes good, and nobody with a dietary rule gets left out.

I'd like to take that worry off your desk. Cakes, cupcakes, snack boxes and hampers, baked fresh for the day, labelled clearly, delivered in the window you need.

Get a quoteSee the menu

What we do for teams and groups

  • Monthly birthday celebrations: one cake or a tray of cupcakes per month, planned ahead, eggless or mixed
  • Meetings and training days: sandwich lunch boxes, puffs, tea-time boxes
  • Festive gifting: Deepavali and Christmas hampers for clients and staff, with your card inside
  • Welcome and milestone gifts: for new joiners, work anniversaries, promotions, retirements
  • Schools: class birthday cupcakes, annual day snacks, staff appreciation
  • Churches, temples and associations: festival orders, tea after events, fundraisers

Why organisers come back

Everything is labelled: veg, non-veg, eggless, contains nuts. Orders arrive in the agreed window, and I'll message you when they leave. You get a single point of contact who actually bakes the food. And because I bake after you order, nothing is from yesterday.

FAQs

Questions people ask

Is there a minimum order for offices?

Message us with your numbers. Smaller office orders are welcome too.

How far ahead should we order for a team event?

For a team of up to 50, order at least 2 days ahead. For festive corporate hampers in large quantities, book weeks ahead.

Can you handle mixed dietary needs in one order?

Yes. Orders can mix vegetarian, non-vegetarian, eggless and reduced-sugar items, each labelled clearly.

Do you deliver to offices across Chennai?

The kitchen is in Madhavaram, so North and Central Chennai offices are easiest. For other areas, message your office location and date and Priya will confirm.
