How Many Puffs and Sandwiches Per Person? Party Quantities, Worked Out

BBy Beth MathewPublished 26 November 20263 min read

For an evening party where snacks replace a meal, plan 8 to 10 savoury pieces per adult. For snacks before dinner, 4 to 6 pieces. For a working lunch, 4 to 6 sandwich quarters plus 1 to 2 bakes per person. Children eat about half an adult portion. Add 10% for unexpected guests, which, in Chennai, you will have.

These are the numbers we use when planning savoury platters.

The one question that changes everything

Is this food the meal, or is it before or after a meal? Get this wrong and you'll either run out at 8 pm or be eating sandwiches for three days.

Snacks before a meal: guests are saving space. Plan light. Snacks instead of a meal: guests arrived hungry and will leave expecting to be full. Tea-time snacks: somewhere in between.

The table

Occasion Savoury pieces per adult Sandwich quarters per adult Sweets per adult
Snacks before dinner 4 to 6 2 1 to 2
Tea-time (4 to 6 pm) 4 to 6 2 to 3 2
Evening party, no meal (6 to 9 pm) 8 to 10 4 2 to 3
Working lunch 1 to 2 4 to 6 1
Kids' party (per child) 1 to 2 2 1 to 2

"Savoury pieces" means puffs, buns, mini pizzas, cutlets, rolls, quiche slices.

Worked examples

30 adults, 6 to 9 pm, no dinner: - 270 savoury pieces (around 9 each), across 4 or 5 kinds - 120 sandwich quarters - 75 sweet pieces - Plus drinks

20 colleagues, working lunch: - 100 sandwich quarters (5 each), mixed veg and non-veg - 30 savoury pieces - 20 cookies or brownies - Fruit

15 children and 15 adults, 4 to 7 pm birthday: - Children: 25 savoury pieces, 30 sandwich quarters, 25 sweets (plus cake) - Adults: 90 savoury pieces, 45 sandwich quarters, 30 sweets - Plus the cake

Variety

More variety means people try a little of everything, so each item can be smaller in quantity. For 30 people, four or five savoury items is plenty. For 100 people, six to eight.

A good mix: one puff, one bun or roll, one baked item (quiche or mini pizza), one fried or crisp item, and sandwiches.

Veg and non-veg

In Chennai, ask. Many gatherings are fully vegetarian; others are mixed. For mixed groups, plan around 60% vegetarian unless you know otherwise, pack veg and non-veg separately, and label clearly.

The 10% rule

Add 10% to every count. Someone will bring a cousin. Someone else will be "just passing". It's Chennai.

FAQs

Questions people ask

How many snacks per person for a party?

For snacks before a meal, plan 4 to 6 savoury pieces per adult. For an evening party where snacks replace dinner, plan 8 to 10 pieces per adult, plus sandwiches and sweets.

How many sandwiches per person for a lunch?

For a working lunch, plan 4 to 6 sandwich quarters per person, plus one or two savoury bakes and something sweet.

How much food do I need for 50 guests?

For an evening party with no dinner, plan around 450 savoury pieces, 200 sandwich quarters and 125 sweets for 50 adults. For snacks before dinner, roughly half that.
