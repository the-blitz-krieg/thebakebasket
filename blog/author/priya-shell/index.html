Priya Shell

Founder and baker at The Bake Basket. Priya has been baking since she was tall enough to reach the counter, and now bakes every Bake Basket order herself in her kitchen in Madhavaram, Chennai. She writes about how things are made, the heritage foods she grew up with, and the small details that decide whether a bake is good or memorable.
