How Far Ahead Should You Order a Custom Cake in Chennai?

PBy Prisha ShellPublished 26 November 20263 min read

For most cakes from a home baker in Chennai, two days' notice is enough; that's what we ask at The Bake Basket. For detailed fondant or tiered cakes, give a week or more. For a wedding cake, start talking four to eight weeks out. And for anything in Deepavali week, Christmas week or the December wedding season, book as soon as you know the date.

That's the short version. Here's why, and what to do if you're reading this on a Thursday for a Saturday party.

Why a home baker needs more notice than a shop

A bakery with a counter is baking all the time. There are sponges in the walk-in fridge, frosting in tubs, and a team of people. When you walk in, they finish a cake that was mostly made already.

A fresh-to-order home baker works differently. At The Bake Basket, nothing gets baked until you order it. That's the whole point: your cake isn't sitting around waiting for you. But it also means each order takes up real time on a real calendar, and there's one oven and one pair of hands.

Here's what actually happens to a custom cake over a few days:

  • Day 1: you share the idea and photos. We talk size, flavour, design, eggless or not.
  • Day 2: the design is confirmed. Any special ingredients, toppers or colours are bought. Fondant figures, if any, are made now so they can dry and hold their shape.
  • Day before: the sponge is baked and cooled completely. A warm sponge melts frosting.
  • The day: filling, frosting, decoration, chilling, the photo for you, and out the door.

Rush that and something gives. Usually it's the decoration, sometimes it's the texture, and occasionally it's the baker's sleep, which you don't want either.

The busy seasons

Some weeks fill up quickly in Chennai and it's worth knowing them in advance.

Deepavali. Hampers and cookie boxes for families, neighbours and offices. Corporate orders start arriving three or four weeks before.

December. Christmas plum cakes, Christmas parties, New Year, and the peak of the wedding and reception season, all at once. If you have a December date, book in November.

Vinayagar Chathurthi. Kozhukattai and modak orders for the same morning. A steamer holds only so many at a time. See our traditional steamed foods page.

Mother's Day, Valentine's Day. Smaller orders, but lots of them on one date.

The school calendar. End of term and the start of the academic year bring class parties and teachers' gifts.

If it's last minute

It happens. The party moved forward, the office forgot, or someone casually mentioned that today is their birthday.

Message anyway. Some things can be done quickly and done well: brownies, a simple cream cake, a bento with a message, a cookie box. What usually can't be rushed is anything with fondant, sculpted shapes, tiers or very specific colours.

A good baker will tell you honestly what's possible. If someone agrees to a three-tier fondant cake for tomorrow, be a little suspicious about where that cake came from.

How to make ordering faster

When you message, send these four things in your first message and you'll save a day of back and forth:

  1. Date, time and delivery area
  2. Number of guests
  3. Flavour, or "help me choose"
  4. Two or three pictures of designs you like, and anything you definitely don't want

Mention eggless, allergies, or a budget up front too. It changes the suggestions.

Have a date in mind? Message us now to hold it, even if the design isn't decided.

FAQs

Questions people ask

How many days before should I order a birthday cake?

At The Bake Basket, please order cakes 2 days ahead, and a week ahead for detailed fondant designs.

Can I order a custom cake one day before?

Sometimes. Simple cream cakes, bento cakes and brownies can often be made at one day's notice, but detailed fondant or tiered designs need more time to be done properly.

When should I book a wedding cake in Chennai?

Start four to eight weeks ahead to allow time for design discussion and a tasting. For December weddings, book earlier, as it's the busiest season.
