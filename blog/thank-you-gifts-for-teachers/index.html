Thank-You Gifts for Teachers That Aren't Another Mug

PBy Prisha ShellPublished 26 November 20263 min read

The thank-you gifts teachers value most are a heartfelt note from the child and something small they can enjoy or share: a tin of cookies, a box of brownies, a loaf of banana bread. Teachers get a lot of mugs, pens and keyrings. They rarely get something homemade with a child's own words on it.

In India, Teachers' Day is 5 September, the birthday of Dr S. Radhakrishnan. The end of the school year and the last day before a teacher leaves are the other big moments. Our brownies and cookies are a popular choice for all three.

What teachers say they value

Ask a few teachers and you'll hear the same thing. The note matters most. A card in a child's handwriting, or a drawing, gets kept for years. Many teachers have a folder of them.

After the note, what's appreciated is something consumable. Teachers have a finite number of shelves. They don't have a finite appetite for good cookies.

Ideas that work

Name cookies. A tin of cookies with "Thank you, Ms Lakshmi" iced on top. Personal, edible, photographable.

A brownie box. Teachers often share with colleagues in the staff room, which makes a box of brownies doubly appreciated.

Banana bread or a tea cake. Something for the teacher's own family at home.

A small basket from the whole class. Parents pool together for one generous basket with cookies, a cake, savoury snacks and a card signed by every child. One thoughtful gift beats thirty small ones.

A plant with a note. Not edible, but lasting.

Something for the staff room. A tray of cupcakes or puffs for all the teachers. A lovely gesture from a class's parents, and the support staff appreciate it too.

A few etiquette notes

  • Check the school's policy. Some schools discourage individual gifts or set a limit. Many prefer a class gift.
  • Keep it modest. Expensive gifts can make teachers uncomfortable.
  • Avoid nuts if the school is nut-free; the staff room counts too.
  • Don't forget the others. Ayahs, bus attendants, security and the office staff look after your child too.

Write the note together

Sit with your child and ask: "What do you want to say to your teacher?" Write down their words, not yours. "Thank you for helping me with fractions" is better than anything a parent could write.

If your child is young, a drawing with a short line ("You are kind") is perfect.

FAQs

Questions people ask

What is a good thank-you gift for a teacher?

A handwritten note from the child with a small consumable gift, such as cookies, brownies or banana bread, is thoughtful and appreciated. A class gift pooled by parents also works well.

When is Teachers' Day in India?

Teachers' Day in India is celebrated on 5 September, the birthday of Dr Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan.

How much should I spend on a teacher's gift?

Keep it modest. Many schools discourage expensive gifts, and teachers value the personal note far more than the price. A class gift lets parents contribute a small amount each.
