Anniversary Cakes for Your Parents: Ideas for 25th, 40th and 50th

PBy Prisha ShellPublished 26 November 20263 min read

For a parents' anniversary, choose a cake that looks elegant rather than flashy, in a flavour their generation loves, at a size that also covers the relatives who will "just drop in". Silver (25th), ruby (40th) and golden (50th) anniversaries each have their own colour, which makes the design easy. The message matters more than the decoration.

The colours, by year

Anniversary Traditional name Colour ideas
25th Silver White and silver, pearl, soft grey
30th Pearl Ivory, cream, pearl details
40th Ruby Deep red, maroon, white with ruby roses
50th Golden Ivory and gold, champagne, gold leaf touches
60th Diamond White, silver, crystal-like sugar work

These aren't rules. If your mother's favourite colour is the peacock blue of her wedding saree, that's a better idea than any list.

Designs that work

The photo cake, done well. An edible print of the wedding photo, framed in piped buttercream. It sounds simple. When the couple sees it, it usually isn't.

The saree border. A buttercream or fondant border that echoes the design of the wedding saree or the veshti's zari. A beautiful, quietly personal detail.

Two tiers, two stories. One tier for the wedding year, one for now. A mini figure of their first scooter, the house they first lived in, the year printed on the side.

Fresh flowers or piped florals. Jasmine-white, rose and gold. Always elegant. (We use food-safe flowers only, kept away from the cake surface where needed.)

Simple and confident. A single-tier cake, smooth ivory buttercream, a gold "50" and their names. Sometimes less really is more.

Flavours for an older crowd

Many parents' generation grew up with fresh cream cakes and fruit flavours, and often find heavy buttercream and rich chocolate too much. Good choices:

  • Pineapple, the 1980s and 90s favourite
  • Butterscotch with crunch
  • Vanilla with fresh fruit
  • Black Forest, the classic party cake of their era
  • Rasmalai, for a dessert that feels Indian and festive

If one or both parents watch their sugar, ask for a reduced-sugar cake, or keep the main cake small and add a box of jaggery or millet bakes for them. There's more on this in our post on choosing a cake when someone has diabetes.

Messages

Anniversary cakes often carry the shortest and most moving messages. A few ideas:

  • "50 years of us"
  • "Still holding hands"
  • "Happy 25th, Amma and Appa"
  • "Thank you for showing us how"
  • "Since 1976"
  • "Our love story starts with yours"

Make it a basket

For parents who don't care much about cake, a basket can mean more. A small anniversary cake, a box of the cookies they love, a loaf of brioche for the morning after, and a handwritten note from each grandchild. It arrives in a real basket, and they'll keep it. See our gift hampers.

FAQs

Questions people ask

What is the colour for a 25th wedding anniversary cake?

The 25th anniversary is the silver jubilee, so white and silver are the traditional colours. Pearl, soft grey and ivory also work well.

What cake flavour do older parents prefer?

Many prefer lighter fresh cream cakes in flavours like pineapple, butterscotch, Black Forest and vanilla with fruit, rather than heavy buttercream or very rich chocolate.

How big should a 50th anniversary cake be?

Count every guest and add a little for visitors. A family gathering of 20 to 25 needs about 2 kg; a hall celebration of 80 to 100 guests needs 7 to 10 kg, often as tiers.
