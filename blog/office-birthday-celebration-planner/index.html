Office Birthdays Without the Stress: A Planner for HR and Admin Teams

PBy Prisha ShellPublished 26 November 20263 min read

The simplest way to run office birthdays is a monthly celebration: one cake or a tray of cupcakes for everyone born that month, on a fixed day, with an eggless option. It's fair, predictable, and easy to budget. For small teams, individual birthdays with a bento cake or cupcakes work well. Either way, confirm diet needs once, set a recurring order, and nominate one person to own it.

We set up exactly this kind of standing order through our corporate and bulk orders page.

Monthly or individual?

Monthly celebrations work best for teams over 20. Everyone born in the month is celebrated together, on, say, the last Friday. One order, one gathering, one set of photos.

Individual birthdays work best for small teams under 15, where each person's day can be marked. A bento cake on their desk in the morning, or a box of cupcakes for the team.

A hybrid: monthly cake for everyone, plus a small personal touch (a card signed by the team, a bento cake) on the actual day.

Set up once, run all year

  1. Build a birthday list with names and dates (with permission; some people prefer not to be celebrated, and that's fine).
  2. Collect diet needs once: vegetarian, eggless, Jain, diabetic, allergies.
  3. Choose a format and day. For example: last Friday of the month, 4 pm, cupcakes plus one cake.
  4. Set a budget per month.
  5. Set a standing order with a baker, with a reminder a week before.
  6. Nominate an owner and a backup.

Eggless or not?

In most Chennai offices, the answer is simply eggless. It means everyone can eat the same cake, and nobody has to ask. Very few people notice the difference. More in eggless birthday cakes.

Cake or cupcakes?

Format Pros Cons
One cake Classic, good for the cutting moment and photos Needs knife, plates and someone to serve
Cupcakes No cutting, no plates, easy for people to grab and go back to desks Less of a "moment"
Both Small cake for cutting plus cupcakes for serving Slightly higher cost

For teams over 30, "both" is usually best.

Quantities

Plan about 80 g of cake per person, or one cupcake each plus 10% extra. See how much cake do you need.

Timing on the day

  • Schedule it in calendars as a short meeting so people actually show up.
  • 4 pm works well: after lunch, before the evening rush.
  • Ask the baker to deliver 30 to 60 minutes before, and keep cream cakes in the pantry fridge.
  • Keep it to 15 minutes. A song, a cut, a photo, back to work.

Beyond cake

Some teams rotate: cake one month, a savoury tea-time box the next, a breakfast spread another. It keeps things fresh and suits people who don't eat sweets.

FAQs

Questions people ask

How should offices celebrate employee birthdays?

Many offices hold a monthly celebration for everyone born that month, with one cake or cupcakes. Smaller teams often mark each birthday individually with a bento cake or cupcakes.

Should office birthday cakes be eggless?

In most Chennai offices, choosing an eggless cake is the simplest option, as it lets every colleague share the same cake.

How much cake do we need for an office of 40 people?

Plan about 3 to 3.5 kg of cake, or one cupcake per person plus a few extra. A small cake for cutting plus cupcakes is a popular combination.
