Corporate Deepavali Gifting in Chennai: Ideas That Get Remembered

PBy Prisha ShellPublished 26 November 20263 min read

The corporate Deepavali gifts people remember are personal, useful, and different from the dry-fruit box every company sends. A food basket with fresh bakes, a mix of sweet and savoury, clear labels, a handwritten card, and delivery to the recipient's home stands out. Plan four weeks ahead for quantities over 50, and confirm diet needs before finalising.

We handle these through our corporate and bulk orders desk.

Why most corporate gifts are forgotten

Every Deepavali, the same box arrives: almonds, cashews, raisins, a candle, a cellophane window. It's not unwelcome, but it isn't memorable either. By the third one, it all blends together.

Gifts that get remembered tend to have one of three things: they're made by someone, they're useful at home, or they come with a personal touch.

What works

Fresh food baskets. Cookies, brownies, a fruit cake, savoury bites, a small traditional sweet. They're shared at home, which is where Deepavali happens.

A real basket. Reusable, kept, and associated with your company every time it's used.

A handwritten card. Even a short line from the team lead makes a difference.

Delivery to home. Many employees and clients are on leave or working from home during Deepavali week. A basket that sits on an office desk over a long weekend isn't much of a gift.

Diet and labelling

In any Chennai office, you'll have vegetarians, eggless families, people avoiding sugar, and the occasional nut allergy. Collect this before ordering, or make the whole order eggless and nut-free with clear labels. Every item should be labelled: veg, eggless, contains nuts, contains gluten.

Logistics

Timeline. Confirm the order and list three to four weeks before Deepavali. Deliveries usually go out in the five days before the festival.

Address list. Collect home addresses with phone numbers in a spreadsheet. Missing PIN codes and landmarks cause most delivery problems.

Delivery days. Spread deliveries over a few days rather than all on the eve of the festival.

Card. Agree on card text early. Personalised cards (with each person's name) take longer than a single message.

Invoicing. Confirm GST invoicing needs and PO numbers in advance.

A word on sustainability

Many companies now avoid plastic-heavy gifts. A cane basket, paper wrapping, glass jars and no plastic windows are easy wins that people notice.

FAQs

Questions people ask

What are good corporate Deepavali gifts in Chennai?

Fresh food baskets with cookies, brownies, savoury snacks and a traditional sweet, packed in a reusable basket with a personal card, are memorable and widely appreciated.

When should companies order Deepavali hampers?

For more than 50 hampers, place the order three to four weeks before Deepavali. Smaller orders need about two weeks.

Should corporate gifts be delivered to the office or home?

Home delivery is usually better during Deepavali week, as many people are on leave or working from home and the gift is shared with family.
