Edible Return Gifts for Kids' Birthday Parties

PBy Prisha ShellPublished 26 November 20263 min read

Edible return gifts are often a better choice than toys: they're eaten instead of forgotten in a drawer, they match the party theme, and they're easy to personalise. Popular options are iced name cookies, mini brownie boxes, a single cupcake in a box, cookie jars, and small treat bags. Order a week ahead for decorated items, and always label anything with nuts or egg.

See our brownies and cookies and cupcakes for the range.

Why edible?

Think about the last ten plastic return gifts your child brought home. How many are still in use? Edible gifts get enjoyed, photographed, and talked about. They also make a lovely last impression of the party.

Ideas, by budget

Small budget - Two or three cookies in a printed bag - A single brownie, individually wrapped, with a sticker

Medium budget - Iced name cookies (each child's name), packed in a bag or box - A mini box of four brownies or cookies - One cupcake in a clear box, in the party's colours

Generous - A cookie jar with a mix of cookies and a name tag - A small box with a cupcake, two cookies and a brownie - A theme set: cookies shaped like the party theme

Matching the theme

Edible gifts can echo the party: dinosaur cookies for a dinosaur party, rainbow sprinkles for a rainbow theme, stars and moons for "little star". Iced cookies are the easiest way to do this. Send your baker the invitation or a photo of the decorations.

Plan for allergies

Ask parents about allergies when they RSVP, or simply make the return gifts nut-free and label egg. A small sticker on each bag ("contains egg, wheat, milk") is responsible and appreciated.

Practical tips

  • Count siblings. Children often bring a brother or sister; order five or ten extra.
  • Keep them cool. Chocolate-coated items melt in a car; choose cookies and plain brownies.
  • Hand them out at the end, not the start, or they'll be eaten before the cake.
  • Add a thank-you tag with the birthday child's name: "Thank you for coming to Aarav's 6th!"

Combine with a non-edible

Some parents like to add a small useful item: a pencil, a mini notebook, a crayon set, a seed packet. Paired with cookies, it feels complete.

FAQs

Questions people ask

What are good return gifts for a kids' birthday party?

Edible return gifts like iced name cookies, mini brownie boxes and cupcakes in a box are popular because they're enjoyed rather than forgotten. Small useful items like crayons or notebooks also pair well.

How far in advance should I order return gift cookies?

Order decorated or name cookies at least a week before the party. Plain cookie bags and brownies need two to three days.

Are edible return gifts safe for children with allergies?

Check with parents about allergies, choose nut-free options where possible, and label every bag with allergens such as egg, wheat and milk.
