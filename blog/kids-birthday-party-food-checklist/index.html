The Kids' Birthday Party Food Checklist

BBy Beth MathewPublished 26 November 20263 min read

For a kids' birthday party, plan three kinds of food: easy finger food for the children, something more filling for the adults, and the cake. Keep the children's food small, mild and familiar (mini sandwiches, puffs, fruit, popcorn, cupcakes), plan two to three savoury pieces per child and three to four per adult, and serve the cake about halfway through, before everyone gets restless.

Here's the checklist we'd give a friend. For savoury bakes and platters, see savoury bakes and sandwiches.

Before you plan the menu

  • [ ] Final headcount: children and adults separately
  • [ ] Party length and time of day (lunch? evening?)
  • [ ] Will adults stay? (In Chennai, usually yes, and they'll eat.)
  • [ ] Allergies and dietary needs: eggless, nut-free, vegetarian, Jain
  • [ ] Venue: home, building clubhouse, play area, hall
  • [ ] Is there a fridge? Ovens? Serving staff?

For the children

Children at parties eat little and often, mostly whatever they can grab while running.

  • [ ] Mini sandwiches (cheese chutney, corn cheese, jam)
  • [ ] Veg puffs or mini pizzas
  • [ ] Popcorn or chips in small cups
  • [ ] Fruit (watermelon, grapes, banana), cut small
  • [ ] Cupcakes or cookies (separate from the cake)
  • [ ] Juice or flavoured milk in small cups or tetra packs
  • [ ] Water, lots of it

Keep it: small, not spicy, not sticky, not messy. Avoid whole nuts and hard sweets for under-fives.

For the adults

In most Chennai parties, adults stay and expect proper food, especially in the evening.

  • [ ] Savoury bakes: puffs, buns, rolls, cutlets
  • [ ] Sandwiches or a platter
  • [ ] Something hot if it's close to a meal time: pav bhaji, pasta, biryani, or a full caterer
  • [ ] Tea and coffee
  • [ ] Something sweet besides cake

The cake

  • [ ] Size for everyone (see how much cake)
  • [ ] Eggless if many guests are
  • [ ] Candles, knife, matches or lighter, cake server, plates
  • [ ] A spot away from windows and the sun
  • [ ] Fridge space until 20 minutes before cutting

Timing

Time What happens
Start Welcome drinks, light snacks for early arrivals
30 to 45 min Games; finger food on the table
Halfway Cake cutting, photos
After cake Main savoury food or meal for adults
End Return gifts at the door

Cutting the cake halfway keeps young children happy (they've been waiting) and gives older guests the chance to leave after cake if they need to.

Quantities, roughly

Item Per child Per adult
Savoury pieces (puffs, buns) 1 to 2 2 to 3
Sandwich quarters 2 3 to 4
Cupcakes or cookies 1 to 2 1
Drinks 2 cups 2 cups plus tea

For an evening party that runs past 7.30 pm, treat it as dinner for adults. More detail in party snack quantities.

Make it easier on yourself

  • Order the savoury bakes and cake from one place, so there's one delivery and one person to call.
  • Ask for everything to be labelled: veg, egg, contains nuts.
  • Keep a box of plain biscuits for the child who won't eat anything else.
  • Have a helper on food duty while you're on host duty.

FAQs

Questions people ask

What food should I serve at a kids' birthday party?

Serve small, mild finger food for children, like mini sandwiches, puffs, fruit and cupcakes, along with more filling savoury food for adults, and the birthday cake.

How much food do I need for a kids' party?

Plan about one to two savoury pieces and two sandwich quarters per child, and two to three savoury pieces plus three to four sandwich quarters per adult. For evening parties, serve a proper meal for adults.

When should the cake be cut at a kids' party?

Cut the cake about halfway through the party, after games and before the main food, when children are still excited and before younger guests get tired.
