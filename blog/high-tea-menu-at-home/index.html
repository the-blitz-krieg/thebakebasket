High Tea at Home: A Menu and How Much to Make

BBy Beth MathewPublished 26 November 20263 min read

A good high tea has three layers: savoury finger food (sandwiches, puffs, quiche), something baked and plain (scones, tea cake, banana bread), and small sweets (brownies, cupcakes, cookies). Plan four to five savoury pieces, one or two plain bakes and two or three sweets per guest for a two-hour afternoon, plus plenty of tea and something cold for Chennai weather.

What "high tea" means here

In Britain, "high tea" originally meant a hearty early-evening meal for working families, and "afternoon tea" was the dainty one. In India, and in Chennai especially, "high tea" has come to mean the dainty version: an afternoon or early-evening spread of small bites with tea. Baby showers, kitty parties, ladies' get-togethers, small engagements, and Sunday afternoons with visiting relatives all qualify.

The menu

Savoury - Finger sandwiches: cucumber and butter, cheese chutney, egg mayo, chicken, paneer tikka - Mini puffs: veg, egg, chicken - Quiche or savoury tarts: spinach and corn, mushroom - Cheese straws - Mini buns or sliders

Plain bakes - Scones with jam and cream, or butter - Tea cake slices: banana walnut, lemon drizzle, marble - Banana bread

Sweet - Mini cupcakes - Brownie squares - Butter cookies, nankhatai - Lemon bars - Small tarts

Drinks - Tea: a strong Indian tea and something lighter, like Darjeeling or a herbal blend - Filter coffee for the guests who will ask for it - Something cold: iced tea, lemonade, rose milk

Quantities per guest (two-hour afternoon)

Item Per guest
Sandwich pieces (quarters or fingers) 3 to 4
Other savoury pieces 1 to 2
Scone or tea cake slice 1 to 2
Sweet pieces 2 to 3
Tea or coffee 2 cups

For 15 guests, that's about 50 sandwich pieces, 20 savouries, 20 plain bakes and 40 sweet pieces.

Presentation

A tiered stand helps, but a long table with platters at different heights works just as well. Keep savoury and sweet separate. Label anything with egg, nuts or meat. Use real cups if you have them; it makes it an occasion.

Timeline

When What
2 days before Order bakes and sandwiches; confirm delivery time
Day before Plan table, set out crockery, chill drinks
2 hours before Deliveries arrive; cream items in the fridge
30 minutes before Lay out savouries and plain bakes
As guests arrive Make tea; bring out sweets after the first round

In Chennai heat

Keep sandwiches covered and in the fridge until the last moment; they dry out fast. Avoid cream-heavy sweets if the room isn't air-conditioned. Puffs can be warmed in an oven for five minutes just before serving.

FAQs

Questions people ask

What food is served at a high tea?

A high tea usually includes finger sandwiches and savoury bites, plain bakes like scones and tea cake, and small sweets such as cupcakes, brownies and cookies, served with tea and coffee.

How much food do I need for a high tea for 20 people?

For 20 guests over two hours, plan around 70 to 80 sandwich pieces, 30 savoury bites, 30 plain bake portions and 50 small sweets.

What is the difference between high tea and afternoon tea?

Traditionally in Britain, high tea was a hearty evening meal and afternoon tea was the dainty spread. In India, high tea usually refers to the dainty afternoon or evening spread.
