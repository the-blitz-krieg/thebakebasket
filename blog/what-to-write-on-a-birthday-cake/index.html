What to Write on a Birthday Cake: 70 Messages That Fit

PBy Prisha ShellPublished 26 November 20263 min read

Keep it short: four to six words on a bento cake, up to eight or ten on a 1 kg round, and always check the spelling of the name twice. The best cake messages sound like something you'd actually say to that person, which is why "Happy Birthday" plus a name almost always works, and why a little private joke works even better.

Below are seventy ideas, sorted by who the cake is for. Steal freely. When you order on WhatsApp, just copy the line in, and we'll pipe it in a steady hand. If it's a small one, our bento cakes are made for short messages.

How much space you really have

Cake Comfortable length
Bento (about 10 cm) 3 to 6 words
500 g round 5 to 8 words
1 kg round 6 to 10 words
2 kg and up A name, a line, and room for a small drawing

Long names take up more room than you think. "Happy Birthday Venkataraghavan" is a full cake on its own.

A few practical tips

Write it exactly as you want it. Capitals, punctuation and emojis included. If you want "Happy Birthday AMMA!!" or "happy birthday amma", say so.

Send the name in writing. Names said over a voice note get misspelt more often than you'd think, especially names with several common spellings, like Sindhu, Sindu and Sindhoo.

Consider a topper for long messages. A printed or acrylic topper can carry more words without crowding the cake.

The note is a second chance. If the message you want is longer, write it on the handwritten Basket Note that comes with every order and keep the cake short.

FAQs

Questions people ask

What is the best thing to write on a birthday cake?

"Happy Birthday" with the person's name is the classic and almost always right. For something more personal, add a short line that sounds like you, such as a nickname, an age or an inside joke.

How many words fit on a cake?

A bento cake fits about three to six words, a 1 kg round cake six to ten. Longer messages fit better on a topper or a card.

What should I write on my mother's birthday cake?

Short, warm lines work best, such as "Best Amma ever", "Happy 60th, Amma" or "To the heart of our home".
