Our story

Hello, I'm Priya

The Bake Basket is a home bakery run by Priya Shell, a mother and a passionate baker. It began the way the best bakeries do: baking for her own family, then for friends, then for friends of friends.

Every cake, brownie, loaf and traditional steamed dish is still made fresh to order, with handpicked ingredients and a mother's care. I choose every ingredient picturing the person who will eat it, and nothing leaves my kitchen unless I'd happily put it in front of my own children.

with love, Priya

FAQs

Questions people ask

Is The Bake Basket a home bakery?

Yes. The Bake Basket is a home bakery in Madhavaram, Chennai, run by founder and baker Priya Shell, who bakes every order herself, fresh to order.

What can I order from The Bake Basket?

Celebration cakes, bento cakes, cupcakes, brownies, cookies, fresh breads, savoury bakes, millet and no-maida healthy bakes, Chennai fusion flavours like filter-coffee cake, gift hampers, and traditional steamed foods such as kozhukattai, vattayappam, ela ada and puttu.

How far ahead should I order a cake?

Please order cakes 2 days ahead. For tiered cakes, hampers and festival orders, message about a week ahead.

Do you make eggless cakes?

Yes. Most cakes, cupcakes, brownies and cookies can be made eggless. Say so when you order on WhatsApp.

Where are you, and do you deliver?

The kitchen is at 229, 1F, AP Achalendra Apartment, Madhavaram Red Hills Highways, Madhavaram, Chennai 600060. Pickup and delivery are by arrangement, with North Chennai neighbourhoods closest.

When are you open?

Monday to Saturday. The Bake Basket is closed on Sundays.

Shall we bake something?

Tell me the occasion, the date and how many people. I will take it from there.
