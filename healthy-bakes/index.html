Healthy bakes

Dessert for the whole table

At most family birthdays there's one person who says "just a small piece for me", and then doesn't have one. Appa, watching his sugar. An aunt who's cutting down. A teenager who's decided maida is the enemy this month.

I bake this part of the menu so they can say yes. Brownies made with ragi, dark and rich enough that the children ask for them too. Cakes sweetened with jaggery or dates. Whole-wheat loaves, millet banana bread, oat cookies with honey.

Lunchbox muffins, veggie muffins, nut-free school cookies.

What these bakes are, and what they aren't

I'd rather tell you plainly than sell you a word.

  • Jaggery, dates and honey are still sugar. They bring flavour and a little more mineral content than white sugar, but your blood sugar still responds to them. A jaggery cake is a better treat. It is not a sugar-free one.
  • "Reduced sugar" means less sugar than my regular recipe, and I'll tell you how much less. It doesn't mean none.
  • Ragi is naturally gluten-free, but many ragi bakes include wheat flour for texture. If you need gluten-free, tell me, and I'll tell you exactly which bakes are and how they're made.
  • Millets add fibre and a deeper, nuttier taste. They make a treat more filling, which usually means people eat less of it without trying.

If someone at home has diabetes, these are the bakes to choose, in a small portion, with their doctor's advice in mind. I'm a baker, not a doctor, and I won't pretend otherwise. Read: Choosing a Cake When Someone in the Family Has Diabetes

FAQs

Questions people ask

Do you make sugar-free cakes in Chennai?

The Bake Basket makes cakes with no refined sugar, sweetened with jaggery or dates, and reduced-sugar cakes. These still contain natural sugars, so they are not sugar-free. Priya will tell you exactly what's in each one.

Is a jaggery cake good for diabetics?

Jaggery is still sugar and raises blood glucose much like white sugar does. A jaggery cake can be a better-quality treat, but anyone with diabetes should eat a small portion and follow their doctor's advice.

Are ragi brownies gluten-free?

Ragi itself is gluten-free, but many ragi recipes include wheat flour. Ask for the gluten-free version if you need it, and Priya will explain how it's made and any cross-contact risk.

Do the healthy bakes taste like regular cakes?

They taste a little different, and that's part of the pleasure. Ragi brownies are deeper and earthier, jaggery cakes have a caramel note, and millet bread is nuttier. They're made to be enjoyed, not tolerated.

Can I order a healthy birthday cake for a child?

Yes. Jaggery or date-sweetened sponges, whole-wheat cakes and reduced-sugar cakes can all be decorated for birthdays.
