Eggless menu

Eggless, and you'd never know

In a lot of Chennai homes, the cake question comes with a second question attached. "Is it eggless?" Sometimes it's Paati. Sometimes it's a fasting day, or a Jain friend, or a child with an egg allergy, or simply a family that has never eaten eggs and never will.

I don't think anyone at a celebration should be handed the "other" cake. So I bake my eggless sponges to stand on their own: soft, springy, moist enough to stay good the next morning. Most guests can't tell which one is eggless. Some of them prefer it.

What can be eggless

  • Cakes: nearly every flavour, from chocolate truffle and butterscotch to red velvet and rasmalai
  • Bento cakes: most flavours
  • Cupcakes: classic, filled and fusion
  • Brownies: fudgy, walnut and most of the range
  • Cookies: butter cookies, nankhatai, choco-chip and more
  • Breads: ask which loaves are egg-free; many breads contain no egg to begin with
  • Heritage foods: kozhukattai, puttu, idiyappam and ela ada are made without egg

What goes in instead

Eggs do a few jobs in a cake. They hold it together, help it rise and keep it moist. In my eggless bakes, those jobs go to things your grandmother would recognise: curd, milk, a little oil or butter, and the right balance of raising agents.

An honest note about my kitchen

My kitchen bakes with eggs too. I use separate bowls and utensils and wash everything well between batches, but I can't promise the trace-free conditions of a kitchen that never sees an egg. If someone has a severe egg allergy, or your family follows strict rules about this, please tell me, and we'll talk it through properly before you order.

FAQs

Questions people ask

Are eggless cakes as soft as cakes with egg?

A well-made eggless cake is just as soft and moist. The Bake Basket's eggless sponges use curd and milk to give a fine, tender crumb, and most guests can't tell the difference.

Can I get an eggless cake for a Jain family?

The cake itself can be made eggless. Strict Jain requirements also cover other ingredients and the kitchen, so message Priya first and she'll tell you honestly what she can and can't promise.

Is eggless the same as vegan?

No. Eggless bakes usually still contain milk, butter or curd. Vegan cakes contain no animal products at all. If you need vegan, ask before you order.

Is it safe to order eggless if my child has an egg allergy?

Eggless bakes are made in a kitchen that also uses eggs, so there is a small risk of cross-contact. For a severe allergy, talk to Priya before ordering.
