Christmas

The Christmas plum cake, soaked and baked the old way

The fruit goes into its jar weeks before Christmas. Raisins, dates, orange peel, cherries, a handful of cashews, all left to soak and darken while the rest of the year carries on. By the time it reaches the batter, the fruit has turned soft and almost jammy, and the whole kitchen smells of cinnamon, clove and nutmeg.

Then comes the caramel. Sugar cooked dark and slow until it's nearly bitter, which is what gives a Kerala plum cake its deep brown colour and that faint burnt-sugar note you can taste under the spice.

FAQs

Questions people ask

How long does a plum cake last?

A well-wrapped rich plum cake keeps for two to three weeks at cool room temperature, and longer in the fridge. Alcohol-soaked cakes keep longer than alcohol-free ones.

Is there an alcohol-free plum cake?

Ask when you book. An alcohol-free plum cake uses fruit soaked in juice, and has the same spice and colour.

What's the difference between plum cake and fruit cake?

In India, "plum cake" usually means a rich, spiced, dark fruit cake made at Christmas. It contains dried fruit like raisins and dates rather than fresh plums; the name comes from the old English use of "plum" for raisins.

When should I order my Christmas plum cake?

Book by mid November. Plum cakes are baked in limited batches and December fills up quickly.
