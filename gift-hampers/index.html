Festive hampers

Love you can carry

In every Indian home there's a basket that never stays home. It goes to the temple and the church. It goes to the hospital ward with a flask of something warm. It goes to the house with the new baby, the neighbour on festival morning, the friend who's had a hard month.

A basket is never for yourself. It's how we carry love to someone else. That's where my bakery's name comes from, and it's why every hamper I make arrives in a real basket, filled with things I've baked for the person you're thinking of. The basket stays with them. One day it'll carry something to someone else.

Festive baskets

Deepavali. Cookie tins, nankhatai, brownies, a small cake, maybe a box of savoury jeera cookies for the uncle who doesn't eat sweets. For family, neighbours and the colleagues you see every day. Christmas. Rich plum cake, vattayappam, gingerbread and butter cookies. Christmas plum cake Pongal, Eid and Onam. Baskets built around the festival's own flavours.

Celebration baskets

Birthday. A bento cake with their name, cupcakes, a few cookies, and a Basket Note. Anniversary. Something for two: a small cake, brownies, a loaf of brioche for the morning after. New baby. Not for the baby, who has milk. For the parents, who haven't slept, haven't cooked and are surviving on biscuits. Banana bread, savoury muffins, soft bread and something sweet that can be eaten with one hand.

Care baskets

Get well. Gentle food for someone recovering: soft breads, idiyappam, mild bakes, nothing heavy. Check the patient's diet first, and I'll build around it. Read: What to Send Someone in Hospital (Food That Actually Helps) Thank you. For the teacher, the doctor, the neighbour who watered your plants for a month. Thinking of you. For grief, a hard week, or no reason you need to explain.

Corporate baskets

Client gifting at Deepavali and Christmas, employee milestones, welcome kits. Same basket, same care, in quantity, with your card inside. Corporate and bulk orders

FAQs

Questions people ask

Can I send a gift hamper to someone in Chennai from another city or country?

Yes. You can order on WhatsApp, pay online and add a personal message. The Bake Basket delivers the hamper with a handwritten note and sends you a photo once it's delivered.

What is in a Bake Basket hamper?

Each hamper is built around the person and occasion, with items like cakes, brownies, cookies, breads or savoury bakes, packed in a real reusable basket with a handwritten Basket Note.

When should I order Deepavali hampers?

Order family hampers at least a week before Deepavali. Corporate quantities need three to four weeks.

Can I choose what goes in the hamper?

Yes. Pick items yourself, or give a budget and a little about the person and Priya will build it for you.

What should I send to someone who just had a baby?

Send food for the parents: soft bread, banana bread, savoury muffins and easy one-handed snacks. New parents rarely have time to cook, and they'll remember who fed them.
