Everyday treats

Cupcakes, one perfect bite at a time

A cupcake has no wrong slice. No one gets the corner piece, no one gets the bit without the cherry. Everybody gets their own little cake, frosted all the way to the edge, and there's something about holding a whole one in your hand that makes adults behave like they're seven.

That's why cupcakes are what I suggest for school birthdays, office treats, and any party where the children will be running around too fast to sit down for a slice.

The flavours

Classic. Vanilla with a soft swirl of buttercream. Double chocolate that's dark all the way through. Red velvet. Butterscotch with a crunch on top.

Filled. Bite into the middle and there's a surprise: Nutella, salted caramel, strawberry jam, or a sharp lemon curd that wakes up the whole cupcake.

Indian fusion. Gulab jamun, with a little piece of jamun hiding inside. Rasmalai, soaked and fragrant with saffron. Kesar pista. Filter coffee, for the grown-ups who are only here for the coffee.

Sets and gifting

  • Boxes of 6, 9 and 12, mixed or single flavour
  • Mini cupcake boxes for tasting tables and office desks
  • Kids' party sets in your theme colours
  • Floral bouquet cupcakes, piped like a bunch of flowers, for Mother's Day and anniversaries
  • Festive boxes for Deepavali, Christmas and Eid

For school birthdays

Many schools now ask for individually portioned treats. Cupcakes are perfect: no knife, no plates, no mess for the teacher. Tell me the number of children and any nut rules at the school, and I'll keep them simple, small and easy to share.

FAQs

Questions people ask

How many cupcakes should I order for a class birthday?

Order one per child plus three or four extra for teachers and the inevitable second helping. For a class of 30, a box of 36 works well.

Can I order mixed flavours in one box?

Yes. Boxes of 9 and 12 can be mixed, for example three each of four flavours.

Do you make eggless cupcakes?

Yes, most cupcake flavours can be made eggless.

What is a cupcake bouquet?

A cupcake bouquet is a set of cupcakes piped to look like flowers and arranged together like a bunch, often used as a Mother's Day, anniversary or thank-you gift.

How long do cupcakes stay fresh?

Buttercream cupcakes are best within a day or two. Keep them in a cool room, or in the fridge in a sealed box if your house is warm, and bring them to room temperature before serving.
