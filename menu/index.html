What's baking

Everything here is baked after you order, so think of this as what I can make for you rather than what's sitting on a shelf. Look for the little marks: E means it can be made eggless, W means it's one of the wholesome bakes with millet, jaggery or less sugar.

If you can't see what you're after, ask. Half my favourite bakes started as someone's "can you make...?"

FAQs

Questions people ask

Which items can be made eggless?

Most cakes, cupcakes, brownies and cookies can be made eggless. Items marked E on the menu are available eggless; ask on WhatsApp if you're unsure about a particular item.

Can I order something that isn't on the menu?

Often, yes. Message Priya with what you have in mind. If it's something she can make well, she'll tell you; if not, she'll say so.

Is the menu the same all year?

The core menu is, but some things are seasonal: mango cakes in summer, plum cake and vattayappam at Christmas, kozhukattai for Vinayagar Chathurthi and ada for Onam.

Do you show prices on the menu?

Because every order is made to your size, design and diet needs, Priya confirms the price in writing on WhatsApp before she starts baking.
