Allergen information

The Bake Basket is a home kitchen, and it handles common allergens every day. Please read this before ordering for someone with an allergy. Last updated: 26 November 2026.

Order on WhatsAppSee the menu

Allergens in our kitchen

Eggs, milk and dairy, wheat and gluten, tree nuts, peanuts, soy and sesame are all used in our kitchen.

What eggless, nut-free and gluten-free mean here

Eggless bakes contain no egg, but they're made in a kitchen that also uses eggs. Nut-free bakes contain no nuts as ingredients, but nuts are used in the same kitchen. Ragi and millet bakes may contain wheat unless we agree a wheat-free version with you. We use separate utensils and clean carefully, but we can't promise a kitchen free of traces.

Tell us before you order

If someone has an allergy, tell us when you order, with as much detail as you can. For severe allergies, we'll talk it through properly, and if we can't make something safely, we'll say so.
